01 · WHO WE ARE

More than healthcare.
It’s the whole picture.

National wellbeing is interconnected. NWM brings youth together to understand issues through evidence, education, responsible discussion and practical action.

◎

Vision

Build a youth-led culture where wellbeing is understood as a measurable, interconnected national priority.

↗

Mission

Study wellbeing through evidence and real-world data, educate young people and develop responsible youth-led solutions.

◈

Principles

Human dignity, evidence, transparency, non-violence, privacy, scientific responsibility and accountability.
